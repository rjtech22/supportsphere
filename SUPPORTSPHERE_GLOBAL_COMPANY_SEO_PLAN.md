# ResolveNexa Global Company SEO Expansion Plan

**Prepared:** 2026-11-01  
**Scope:** Walmart, eBay, FedEx, UPS, DHL, Airbnb, Booking.com, and the existing Amazon country pages.  
**Canonical production host:** `https://resolvenexa.vercel.app`  
**Verification rule:** publish a company-country page only when the official support route, applicable country, source URL, and verification date are recorded. Do not publish a static phone number unless an official company source displays it for that country.

## Executive summary

- None of the seven new target companies currently exists in `globalCompanies`, `priorityCompanies`, or the inspected Supabase-backed content loader. Adding them will not duplicate a current static company-country record.
- Amazon already has four separate verified records: United States, India, United Kingdom, and Canada. These records correctly use country-specific domains and order-linked support routes.
- Phase 1 should add seven **United States** pages because official US support sources were verified for each company. Do not automatically clone those records into the UK, Canada, Australia, India, or other countries.
- Each company-country page should consolidate company-support, phone/contact, refund, delivery, tracking, and escalation intent where relevant. Slight keyword variations should not create separate pages.
- Phone-number keywords are eligible only for Walmart US, FedEx US, UPS US, DHL Express US, and Airbnb US based on the official sources reviewed below. eBay and Booking.com should explain their official signed-in/local contact flows without publishing an unverified universal number.
- Amazon pages should be strengthened in place. No additional Amazon URL is needed until a genuinely country-specific official support dataset is verified.

## Current implementation findings

### Data and routes

- Static company records are merged from `lib/global-data.ts` and `lib/priority-companies.ts`.
- Supabase can override or add records through `company_country_support`, `support_contacts`, and `official_sources` via `lib/database-content.ts`.
- Public company routes use `/{locale}/{country}/companies/{slug}`.
- A database company is published only when the support record is published and verified, has `verified_at`, contains an official source, and resolves an official support URL.
- Company metadata is generated in `app/[locale]/[[...segments]]/page.tsx`.
- Company keyword rows are generated in `lib/keyword-strategy.ts`. `contact number`, `toll-free number`, live-chat, and email phrases are generated only when the corresponding verified field exists.

### SEO controls already in place

- Each locale receives a self-referencing canonical URL.
- English, Tamil, Hindi, French, and `x-default` alternatives are generated by the shared localization helpers.
- `app/sitemap.ts` includes only verified company records with at least one official source.
- Empty country and category pages are `noindex,follow`; search-results pages are also `noindex,follow`.
- The sitemap is generated from the merged published company dataset, so a verified new record automatically adds its company, country, and useful category paths.

### Keyword mapping changes required

`lib/keyword-strategy.ts` currently generates only general company-support/channel phrases from company fields. Add company-problem rows only when the corresponding content field exists:

- `refundCancellation` -> `[Company] refund not received`
- `deliveryTracking` -> `[Company] package delivered but not received` and `[Company] tracking not updating`
- non-empty `complaintSteps` or `escalation` -> `[Company] customer support not responding` and `[Company] complaint escalation`

The generated rows should target the same company URL. Do not create one route per phrase. Keep search volume and competition `null` until a reliable source provides actual values.

## Required content structure for each new page

1. Unique country-scoped title, meta description, and H1.
2. Official website, help center, and support/contact route.
3. Verified contact channels, each with source URL and `verifiedAt` date.
4. Clear statement when no verified static phone or email is published.
5. Refund/cancellation or claims guidance only where applicable.
6. Delivery/tracking or booking-specific help based on the company type.
7. Complaint steps and escalation using the official case/order/booking flow.
8. Common issues and original FAQs that accurately summarize the cited official process.
9. Links to `refund-not-received`, `package-delivered-not-received`, `tracking-number-not-working`, `customer-support-not-responding`, `how-to-escalate-customer-complaint`, and the complaint-letter tool only where relevant.
10. Related companies from the same country/category.

## Duplicate keyword and cannibalization risks

| Risk | Impact | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Separate pages for `customer service`, `customer support`, `contact number`, and `help center` | Thin pages compete for the same intent | Consolidate all phrases on one company-country page |
| Generic `/companies/fedex` plus `/us/companies/fedex` | Conflicting canonicals and location ambiguity | Keep only locale-country architecture; legacy routes should permanently redirect |
| Publishing cloned US pages for UK/CA/AU | Misleading phone numbers and duplicate content | Require localized official sources and meaningful content differences before publishing |
| DHL vs DHL Express vs DHL eCommerce | Wrong support route and keyword overlap | Label the division explicitly; create a separate DHL eCommerce record only after independent verification |
| eBay/Booking.com phone-number phrases | Users may expect a static number that the official source does not publish universally | Explain the official signed-in/booking-specific contact flow and never invent a number |
| General refund guide vs company refund sections | Possible intent overlap | General guide owns generic queries; company page owns `[Company] + refund` queries and links back to the guide |
| Multiple Amazon country pages | Cross-country duplicate content | Use country domain, policies, channels, examples, metadata, and internal links specific to each market |
| Tamil/Hindi/French locale copies with untranslated company detail | Near-duplicate multilingual pages | Index a translated locale only when its editorial sections are genuinely translated and reviewed |

## Pre-publication acceptance checks

- No duplicate `(country, slug)` record exists.
- Every contact value has an official source URL and verification date.
- Every phone number is explicitly scoped to the page country and relevant company division.
- Company facts are original summaries, not copied help-center articles.
- Title, description, H1, and FAQs are unique and country-appropriate.
- Canonical is self-referencing; hreflang alternatives point to equivalent translated pages only.
- The company route returns 200 and appears once per locale in the generated sitemap.
- No empty country/category route becomes indexable.
- Internal company-to-guide and guide-to-company links resolve.
- Production build, company audit, link check, and sitemap/hreflang checks pass before deployment.

## Implementation decision

Proceed with seven US records plus in-place Amazon improvements. Treat all other country-company combinations as data-verification work, not automatic SEO page generation. This gives ResolveNexa high-intent coverage without publishing fake numbers, thin keyword variants, or duplicated international pages.
